## Frequently Asked Questions

<div style="margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">
  <p style="font-weight: 600; margin-bottom: 0.5rem;">How does AI work with MCP (Claude Code, Cursor, Codex)?</p>
  <p style="color: var(--color-muted-foreground);">When you use Kyomi as an MCP server, the AI runs in your tool (Claude Code, Cursor, Codex). Kyomi provides the knowledge layer, database connections, and curated dashboards. Your existing AI subscription covers the LLM cost — there's no additional AI charge from Kyomi.</p>
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">
  <p style="font-weight: 600; margin-bottom: 0.5rem;">What if I have both a key and bundles?</p>
  <p style="color: var(--color-muted-foreground);">Your API key is used first. Token bundles are the fallback — useful for team members who don't have their own key, or as a backup if your key hits rate limits.</p>
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">
  <p style="font-weight: 600; margin-bottom: 0.5rem;">Is there a free trial?</p>
  <p style="color: var(--color-muted-foreground);">Yes. Cloud has a 30-day free trial with full access to every feature. No credit card required. After 30 days, it's $5/user/month. Desktop and self-hosted are free forever.</p>
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">
  <p style="font-weight: 600; margin-bottom: 0.5rem;">What's the difference between desktop and self-hosted?</p>
  <p style="color: var(--color-muted-foreground);">Desktop is a single binary that uses SQLite — no database to set up, just download and run. Self-hosted uses Docker with PostgreSQL for multi-user teams. Both are free and include all features.</p>
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">
  <p style="font-weight: 600; margin-bottom: 0.5rem;">Does my data leave my database?</p>
  <p style="color: var(--color-muted-foreground);">No. Kyomi queries your database directly and the AI sees max 20 rows of results per query. Your data stays where it is.</p>
</div>
