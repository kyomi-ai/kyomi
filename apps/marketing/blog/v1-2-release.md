We're excited to announce Kyomi v1.2, bringing two major capabilities that change how teams interact with their data: **Kyomi Watch** for autonomous monitoring and **Slack integration** for data insights where you work.

## Kyomi Watch: Your Always-On Data Guardian

Stop checking dashboards. Let Kyomi watch for you.

Kyomi Watch is an AI-powered monitoring system that runs on your schedule—hourly, daily, or weekly—and alerts you only when something noteworthy happens.

### How It Works

1. **Describe what matters** in plain English: "Alert me if revenue drops more than 10%"
2. **AI watches 24/7** on your schedule, analyzing data and deciding when to alert
3. **Get notified** via Slack, email, or in-app when conditions are met

<img src="/images/watch-creation.png" alt="Watch creation interface" style="max-width: 100%; border-radius: 0.5rem; border: 1px solid var(--color-border); margin: 1.5rem 0;" />

### Two Modes for Different Needs

**Alert Mode** - Conditional notifications when thresholds are crossed:
- "Notify me when error rate exceeds 5%"
- "Alert if customer churn spikes unusually"

**Report Mode** - Scheduled summaries delivered regularly:
- "Send me daily revenue by region every morning"
- "Weekly engagement summary every Monday at 9 AM"

<img src="/images/slack-alert.png" alt="Slack alert notification showing anomaly detection" style="max-width: 500px; border-radius: 0.5rem; border: 1px solid var(--color-border); margin: 1.5rem 0;" />

### Investigate with Full Context

When an alert fires, click "Investigate" to continue in chat with all context preserved—the SQL queries, AI reasoning, and data samples are ready for deeper analysis.

## Slack Integration: Insights Without Leaving Slack

Your team lives in Slack. Now your data does too.

### @kyomi in Any Channel

Mention @kyomi with a question and get instant answers:

> @kyomi what was last week's conversion rate?

<img src="/images/slack-mention-chart.png" alt="Asking @kyomi in Slack with chart response" style="max-width: 500px; border-radius: 0.5rem; border: 1px solid var(--color-border); margin: 1.5rem 0;" />

### Charts Render in Threads

Visualizations appear as images right in Slack—no screenshots, no exports, no context switching. Share insights with your team in the flow of conversation.

### Seamless Handoff

Every Slack conversation syncs to Kyomi web. Click "Continue at Kyomi" to pick up where you left off with full context preserved. Or add messages in Kyomi web and watch them appear back in Slack.

### Getting Started

1. Install the Kyomi app in your Slack workspace
2. Run `/kyomi connect` to link your account
3. Start asking questions with @kyomi
